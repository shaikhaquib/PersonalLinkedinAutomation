Most teams still believe you only need to design for the latest Android SDK when a device ships with it out of the box.

When new OS versions surface in developer consoles, we rush to audit edge-to-edge constraints, permission changes, and background restrictions. But ignoring how older mid-range hardware handles newer runtimes leads to silent ANRs long before the update hits the mainstream.

Targeting newer Android versions requires auditing your background process limits and process LRU eviction policies, not just updating compileSdk.

How early do you start testing upcoming Android versions against your existing production baseline?
